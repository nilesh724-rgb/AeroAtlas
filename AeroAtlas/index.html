A LITTLE MORE ALTITUDE

Did you know?

Aviation is full of clever engineering, surprising records and details that are easy to miss from the cabin window.

01 / ENGINEERING
◉
AERODYNAMICS

Those wing tips have a job.

Winglets and other wing-tip devices help reduce the strength of wingtip vortices, which can lower induced drag and improve efficiency.

02 / NAVIGATION
◎
THE SHORTEST PATH

Flights follow a curve.

On a globe, the shortest path between two points is a great-circle route. It can look curved on a flat map.

03 / AIRPORTS
▤
RUNWAY NUMBERS

Look at the runway.

Runway numbers are broadly based on the magnetic direction of the runway, rounded to the nearest ten degrees, with the last digit removed.
